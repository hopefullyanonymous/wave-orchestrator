The four verdicts

Pass means it did what the step says. Fail means it did something else, and the note should say what.

Blocked means the step could not be performed as written, because a button is not there or a dialog closes when it should not. That is a defect in the step rather than in the app, and it is worth knowing separately.

Works, but means the step passed exactly as described and you want the thing itself to be different. It is not a failure and it does not count against the build. It goes to the backlog.

If a section flagged Touches your data looks wrong, stop there and say so before continuing.
